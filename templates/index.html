Technical Architecture

Model Architecture & Data Integrity Standards

Deep dive into data leakage safeguards, target imbalance mitigation, and multiclass decision boundary formulation.

Anti-Data-Leakage Enforcement

In credit datasets, columns like total_payment, last_payment_date, and next_payment_date reflect post-origination loan repayment actions. In practical deployment, these attributes do not exist when a new applicant walks in.

Our pipeline explicitly removes all post-origination fields, ensuring the models rely exclusively on origination-time indicators: loan_amount, installment, int_rate, annual_income, dti, and verification flags.

The Imbalance "Accuracy Paradox"

Because 83.3% of applicants naturally pay off their loans, an algorithm that blindly approves 100% of loans achieves an astounding 83.3% raw accuracy while catching zero defaulters.

In lending, a default causes an immediate severe capital loss. By switching our benchmark criteria to Balanced Accuracy and Macro F1-Score, we penalize models that fail to flag minority defaulters.
